Problem Statement

GloMark is a global field sales organisation operating across six countries: Australia, Canada, France, Germany, the United Kingdom, and the United States. As the business grew, stakeholders found it increasingly difficult to monitor sales performance, identify profit drivers, and make time-informed decisions across multiple markets.

There was no centralised view of which seasons, weekdays, products, or geographies were driving the majority of profit, which made planning reactive instead of proactive.

Objective
Build a two-page interactive Excel dashboard that gives leadership a full-picture view of sales, profit, and transaction data from 2005–2008.
Identify the specific time periods, products, and markets generating disproportionate profit.
Enable drill-down analysis by country, year, quarter, and weekday through dynamic filtering.
Surface actionable intelligence for product investment, staffing, and promotional strategy.
Tools Used
MS Excel, for multi-sheet dashboard design and visual layout
Pivot Tables & Formulas, for custom calculations covering profit margin, weekday/weekend splits, and year-on-year comparisons
Interactive slicers, providing country and year filters for cross-dimensional drill-down
KPI cards, donut charts, line charts, clustered bar charts, and map visuals
Step-by-Step Approach
Step 1
Data collection & cleaning
Gathered raw transactional sales data across 6 countries and 4 years. Removed duplicates, standardised date formats, and structured the data for multi-dimensional analysis.
Step 2
Data modelling & formulas
Created calculated columns for weekday classification, profit margin %, and year-on-year growth. Built relationships between the date, product, and transaction tables using Excel formulas and pivot tables.
Step 3
Page 1: Time analysis
Designed the high-level dashboard with KPI cards, a monthly trend line, weekday vs. weekend profit split, quarterly comparison bars, and year-over-year revenue chart.
Step 4
Page 2: Detailed dashboard
Built product profitability rankings, colour-based profit breakdown, pricing tier analysis, geographic profit map, and customer age-group contribution visuals.
Key Insights
Time pattern
Q2 is the strongest quarter at $39M profit (31%). Wednesday & Thursday alone contribute 43.8% of total profit, a useful staffing and scheduling insight.
Geography
The US and Australia jointly account for 62.7% of global profit, with just two of six markets carrying the majority of the business.
Product intelligence
The Mountain-200 series drives 24.8% of all profit. Products priced above $150 generate 95.4% of total profit ($120.52M).
Customer demographics
Customers aged 50+ contribute 41.8% of profit, making them the largest single age group, often overlooked by mainstream marketing strategies.
Recommendations
1
Concentrate sales effort on Q2 and mid-week
Schedule key pitches, proposals, and campaigns around Q2 and Wednesday–Thursday windows to align with proven peak profit periods.
2
Prioritise the Mountain-200 product line
Increase inventory, marketing spend, and sales training around the Mountain-200 series given its outsized contribution to overall profit.
3
Audit underperforming markets
Canada, France, Germany, and the UK collectively generate 37.3% of profit despite representing 4 of 6 markets, so a strategic review of investment vs. return is warranted.
4
Align marketing to the 50+ demographic
With the 50+ age group driving 41.8% of profit, campaigns and messaging should be meaningfully tailored to this segment rather than defaulting to younger audiences.
Conclusion

This project demonstrates how a well-structured Excel dashboard can transform millions of raw sales records into clear, prioritised business intelligence. From identifying mid-week profit concentration to uncovering the Mountain-200 product effect, every visual was built around a decision GloMark's leadership actually needs to make. The result is not just a reporting tool; it is a strategic asset that turns data into direction.
