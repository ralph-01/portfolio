Problem Statement

A furniture business selling across the United States had four years of order data spread over 48 states and 131 cities, shipped through four different service levels. As the volume of orders grew, it became difficult to see what was really driving sales and where delivery was working well.

There was no single view showing which product categories, months, shipping modes, and regions were producing the most sales, or how quickly orders were reaching customers, which made planning harder than it needed to be.

Objective
Build an interactive one page Excel dashboard that gives a full picture of furniture sales, profit, and quantity from 2014 to 2017.
Show how orders are shipped, how long they take to arrive, and which shipping modes are used the most.
Enable filtering by customer segment, region, and year, with year on year comparison on the KPI cards.
Highlight the top selling categories, states, and cities, along with the peak sales month.
Tools Used
MS Excel, for dashboard design and visual layout
Pivot Tables & Formulas, using GETPIVOTDATA, INDEX MATCH, VLOOKUP, and IFERROR for KPI cards, year on year change, and dynamic insight titles
Interactive slicers, providing segment, region, and year filters for drill down analysis
KPI cards, line chart, donut chart, bar charts, and a filled map visual
Step-by-Step Approach
Step 1
Data review
Reviewed 2,121 order lines covering 1,696 orders, 649 customers, and 48 states from 2014 to 2017. Confirmed there were no blank values or duplicate rows, and used the Duration and Month fields to support delivery time and monthly trend analysis.
Step 2
Pivot tables & formulas
Built ten pivot tables on an Analysis sheet to summarise sales, profit, quantity, shipping mode, delivery duration, category, state, and city. Used GETPIVOTDATA, INDEX MATCH, and IFERROR formulas to power the KPI cards, year on year change, and the dynamic chart titles.
Step 3
Header, filters & KPI cards
Designed the dashboard header with segment, region, and year slicers, and added KPI cards for Sales, Profit, and Quantity, each with a year on year indicator.
Step 4
Charts & visuals
Added a monthly sales trend line, a shipping mode donut, a sales by category bar chart, a delivery duration chart, a shipping distribution map by state, and a top five cities bar chart, all in a consistent green theme.

Key Insights
Time pattern
October is the peak sales month at $75.5K, followed by April ($70.0K) and June ($69.1K). January and August are the weakest months at about $51K each.
Product intelligence
Chairs lead with $335.0K in sales, 45.1% of the total, and also earn the most profit at $32.7K. Tables and Bookcases bring in sales but run at a loss of $13.6K and $10.2K.
Shipping
Standard Class carries 61% of all orders, while Same Day is only 5%. About 21% of orders are delivered in 0 days, and 54% arrive within two days.
Geography
Colorado is the top selling state at $30.2K and Washington, D.C. is the top selling city at $19.9K. Sales are spread evenly across the four regions at about 25% each, but the Central region earns the most profit at $9.9K.
Recommendations
1
Review pricing and costs on Tables and Bookcases
Together they make up 41.7% of sales but lose about $23.8K in profit. A review of pricing, discounts, and product costs is needed so these lines stop eroding the profit earned by Chairs.
2
Build on Chairs and Furnishings
Give more marketing and inventory focus to Chairs, and look at growing Furnishings, which has the highest profit margin at 13.6%.
3
Plan around October and lift the slow months
Prepare stock, staffing, and promotions ahead of the October peak, and use targeted offers in January and August, the two weakest months.
4
Improve delivery times on Standard Class
With 61% of orders using Standard Class and 46% of all orders taking three days or more to arrive, faster fulfilment on this mode would improve the customer experience where most orders sit.
Conclusion

This project shows how a well organised Excel dashboard can turn more than two thousand order lines into a clear view of sales, shipping, and regional performance. From the October peak to the strength of Chairs and the losses on Tables and Bookcases, every visual was built around a question a furniture business needs answered. The result is not just a report; it is a working tool that turns data into direction.
